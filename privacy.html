Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy.txt

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Burn CD is a small fun project. It has no accounts, no ads and no tracking of its own. This page explains what happens to your information.

What we collect

Nothing is stored on our servers. We do not collect your name, email or location, and we do not use cookies.

Searches and previews

When you search for a song, your search words are sent from your browser to the Apple iTunes Search API and, if that finds nothing, to the Deezer API. Song previews and cover art are loaded from Apple and Deezer. Their own privacy policies apply to that traffic.

Share links and notes

Your CD is saved inside the link itself (the song IDs and your note). Anyone who has the link can read the note. Please do not put private or personal information in a note.

Fonts and hosting

Fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when the page loads. The website host may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and time of visit) for security.

Children

Burn CD is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.
